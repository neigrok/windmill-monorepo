Refunds

Updated July 2026

Windmill is free to use — you only ever pay for tending, the AI that builds your tree. The short version on that: 30 days, no argument. If Windmill Pro isn’t earning its $12, write to us and we’ll give it back.

The 30-day guarantee

Ask for a refund within 30 days of a payment and we’ll refund that payment in full. You don't have to justify it — "it wasn't for me" is a complete reason. Write to hello@windmill.works from the address on the account, and we'll confirm when it's done.

Refunds are returned to the card or method you paid with. Depending on your bank, the money typically reappears within five to ten working days once the refund is issued.

Cancelling

Cancel any time from account settings — no email, no retention call. Cancelling stops the next renewal; your subscription runs to the end of the period you’ve already paid for, and you keep Pro until then.

After 30 days

Past the 30 days we don't refund a period that's already run, because you had the thing you paid for. Cancel and you won't be charged again. If something went genuinely wrong — you were billed twice, or charged after cancelling, or the service was broken for you — that isn't a refund request, it's our mistake, and we'll fix it whenever you tell us.

Nothing you made is ever deleted

Lapsing or refunding never destroys your work, and nothing that is free today will move behind a plan later — privacy included. Everything stays exportable from account settings at any time, on any plan.

Who takes the payment

Payments are processed by Paddle, who act as the seller of record for Windmill — your receipt and card statement will show Paddle rather than Windmill. Refunds go through them too, which is why we handle the request rather than asking you to. Paddle's own buyer terms apply alongside ours, and where a statutory right gives you more than this policy does, the statutory right wins.

Questions

Write to hello@windmill.works — a person reads it, and a person answers.
