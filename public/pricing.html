The details

Cancel any time, from account settings. Your subscription runs to the end of the period you've paid for, and nothing renews after that.

30-day money-back guarantee. If Windmill isn't for you, write to us within 30 days of paying and we'll refund it — see the refund policy.

What happens to a private tree if you stop paying. Nothing is deleted, ever. Trees you made private stay private and stay yours to read and export; you just can't make new ones private until you're back on Pro.

Prices are in US dollars and shown before tax. Any sales tax or VAT is calculated at checkout for where you are. Payments are handled by Paddle, who act as the seller of record — your receipt and card statement will say Paddle.

Questions about any of this? Write to hello@windmill.works — a person reads it.
