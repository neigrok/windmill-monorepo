What we'll charge for later

Two things, both of which cost us money every time they're used — which is the honest test for whether something should be paid:

Heavy use. Shaping a pasted document into a plan runs a model on our bill. Doing it occasionally will stay free; doing it constantly is where a plan starts.

AI inside Windmill. Today an AI tool can build your trees only if you run one on a desktop. Bringing that into the site itself — so you can do it from a phone, with no agent to install — is the piece worth paying for.

We briefly charged for private trees. That was wrong: it meant new trees had been quietly changed to link-visible so that privacy could be sold back, and privacy is not a thing to hold hostage. It's free, it's the default again, and nothing that is free today will move behind a plan later.

When there is something to buy it will be billed by Paddle as seller of record, with a 30-day money-back guarantee — see the refund policy.

Questions? Write to hello@windmill.works — a person reads it.
