**Implementation — source complete; native acceptance pending**
- `platform/you/AccountDeletion.kt` owns the sealed immutable close operation, captured identity/replica and cleanup phases. Unreadable or pending records fence account writes, transitions and normal auth/sync startup before any credential bootstrap.
- `platform/you/DeleteAccount.kt` and `YouSheet.kt` implement 27a–g and D1–D5 using the approved Figma assets and native Material controls. Pending You retains history access and Check deletion; it offers no sign-in or sign-out action.
- Confirmed closure discards only the pinned replica, removes that owner’s workout controls and Coach archive/photos, then clears the original credentials and pending record. Failure retries the owed phase without a new close or an ended handle. Same-account replacement credentials and replicas are refused; another account is preserved.
- Coach owner epochs retire old replies, photos and routine confirmations across cleanup and process restart. Only a fresh verified sign-in can reopen a restored account; its epoch remains advanced. The anonymous installation identity/binding and unrelated owner data remain intact.
- The backend enforces the 30-day restoration grace and 35-day result receipt. `closesMs` retains milliseconds; its ISO `closingOn` rendering is validated at second precision.

**Backend prerequisite — `account-delete-reconciliation`**
- Owner: platform/auth backend. [The recoverable deletion contract](../backend/AUTH.md#recoverable-deletion) defines the implemented wire API; the existing grace enforcement remains in place.
- Send authenticated `DELETE /v1/me` with the confirmed `account`, a persisted random UUID `operationId`, a 32-byte random `proof` encoded as 64 lowercase hex characters, and one immutable `requestedAtMs`. The server admits a new operation within five minutes of its clock and atomically commits closure, receipt and session/OAuth revocation. Identical replays return the original deadline; they never reclose a restored account.
- Recover through session-independent `POST /v1/account-deletion/result` with that same JSON tuple. The phone seals the proof; the server retains only its domain-separated hash. The capability reads only that result, never restores an account, and grants no account reads/writes. Receipts survive account erasure and expire after 35 days. Responses are private/no-store; credentials never enter URLs, logs or events.
- `state=closed` proves that operation committed. `pending` means an unknown outcome, not a persisted server job. `unavailable` (wrong, missing or expired receipt) proves neither success nor failure. A direct malformed/account-mismatch/clock-ahead refusal proves only that attempt made no mutation; it cannot disprove an earlier possibly committed attempt. Keep the original tuple when recovering; never change its timestamp to bypass expiry. The body-only legacy `DELETE /v1/me` has no recoverable receipt.

**Behavior**
- 16b's last row **Delete account** (error role) pushes a page in You (`platform/you/DeleteAccount.kt`), signed in only. Signed out there is no such row.
- 27a: back bar *Delete account*; title *Delete your account?*; four facts, each with its kit symbol (`delete`, `move_item`, `schedule`, `download`):
  - *This deletes your Windmill account and its copy in the cloud: your roadmaps, journal pages and gym log.*
  - *Every device signs out, and connected tools lose access, right away.*
  - *For 30 days, you can sign in again to bring it all back. After that, it is deleted for good.*
  - *To keep a copy, export it first at windmill.works.*
  - *Type your email to confirm*, an outlined field *Email*; **Delete account** (filled, error role, at 38 % and off until the typed email matches the account's, case-insensitive and trimmed); **Cancel** (text button) goes back.
- 27b: the email typed (*lifter@example.com*), keyboard up, the button on.
- 27f, 27g: when the phone holds changes the account has not confirmed, a fifth fact row, last, with `cloud_off`: *3 changes on this phone haven’t been confirmed by your account. They are deleted too.* (*1 change on this phone hasn’t been confirmed by your account. It is deleted too.*) The live count includes held entries that the prepared sign-out releases, plus `ready + sent + pending` device work; the row goes when it reaches 0. No extra choice.
- Pin the confirmed account id, operation id and private recovery proof in a durable pending-deletion record before sending. The request names that account id, never the typed email. Lock account transitions while starting the operation, and never attach a late result to whichever account is currently selected.
- 27c: **Deleting…** with the kit circular progress; the field off; Back and Cancel disabled during the active attempt. First obtain the engine's sign-out session/flush, then submit the close operation. Until the outcome is known, fence new writes for that account.
- A confirmed closed receipt creates a durable owed-cleanup record for that exact account and returned deadline. Finish its held sign-out with Discard, remove its owner-scoped workout controls, Coach archive/photos and credentials and clear the owed record only after cleanup succeeds. A cleanup failure leaves the record recoverable and the affected account unavailable; retry performs cleanup, not another close.
  - If the app restarted, reopen the cleanup session for that account. If a different account is active, discard only the original account's dormant data; never finish a sign-out or clear storage belonging to the new account. Follow the existing engine lifecycle APIs and the account-pinned iOS cleanup pattern.
- 27d appears only after a confirmed closed receipt **and** successful local cleanup: *Your account is deleted* / *Sign in before Nov 21 to bring it back.* (the actual `closingOn`, localized) / **Done** → signed-out gym (16a). Do not invent the deadline or claim success from an unauthorized response.
- 27e is for a definitive not-closed/refused result or a proven failure before submission. Preserve typed email; use the appropriate error sentence and **Try again**. Cancel the engine's sign-out hold on this path. `account-mismatch` returns to You after releasing the hold, preserving both accounts.
- A timeout, lost response, 5xx after possible mutation, or 401 on retry is an **unknown closure outcome**. A successful close revokes the session before its response; 401 alone proves neither an account switch nor deletion. Keep the operation record, recover the same result using the backend receipt contract, and do not send a fresh close operation or sign in to check (sign-in restores a closing account).
- Every sign-out session has a disposition: finish Discard after proven closure; cancel on proven no-close, explicit pre-send cancellation or when releasing a transient session at a lifecycle boundary. An unresolved operation retains its durable write fence and recovery record independently of that transient hold, so process death neither leaks an in-memory hold nor resumes account writes. Resolve/reacquire the correct session during recovery.
