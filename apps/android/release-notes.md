# Windmill Gym for Android 0.10.0

Routine plans and training history now have their own homes.

- A routine opens as a sheet over the list, with its movements, targets, Start workout and Edit
  routine. Past workouts and movement records live in the Log.
- The Log places new bests, weigh-ins and completed training months between your workouts, with
  at most one moment per week. Expand a best to see its chart and open the movement record.
- Creating a movement for a routine includes its target on the same sheet. Sets, reps and kg use
  shared stepper controls, with Ramp up and Vary by set for individual targets.
- Rest timers and alerts are removed. The ongoing workout notification keeps the routine,
  movement, rack numbers and set counter, with Log set, Open and Hide actions.

## Installation

The retained release signing identity supports Android’s normal in-place update from 0.9.3.
Keep local records by installing this APK over the existing app.

Historical APKs through 0.7.1 used different certificates and cannot update in place. Uninstalling
removes records saved only on that phone; preserve those records before changing the installation.
Signing in alone does not transfer anonymous records.
