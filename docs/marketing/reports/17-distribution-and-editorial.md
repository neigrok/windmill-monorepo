# 17 · Build a useful internet presence

The founder owns this queue under the scale of **eight owner hours a week, with spending only by approved proposal**. The [growth options report](18-growth-options.md) owns the broader portfolio, strategic choices and resource alternatives. Engineering owns HTML and publishing fixes; the release owner supplies build evidence and store readiness. The [strategy](../strategy.md#launch-contract) owns release scope. The [marketing execution tree](https://windmill.works/#/app/t_970fc0a989207ce2) owns task progress and outcomes. This is planned work, not a record of campaigns sent or articles published. Who does each piece follows [the three marks](../strategy.md#what-the-owner-does-and-what-agents-do): agents research, draft for the product and build; the owner rules, approves and writes what is his own. Where an agent prepares a piece, the owner's hours in that block go to reading and approving it.

## Proposed channel choices

Start with **one 3–6 minute query-led YouTube tutorial** that answers the same question as the usable artifact. The title, demonstration and destination must concern one job. Short cuts serve discovery of that tutorial; they are not assumed to deliver direct outbound clicks. This is a channel hypothesis, not evidence of an existing Windmill account or audience. Confirm account access, feature eligibility and the actual route before production. Whether Windmill gets a video channel is decided when the first tutorial exists. There is no X or Bluesky account, and a Reddit product account is opened only when an approved place allows a product to answer.

YouTube makes URLs in Shorts descriptions and comments non-clickable. A channel profile link or a related-video link can provide an onward route; external links in long-form descriptions require advanced-feature access. Test the complete path on the intended account and device: short → related tutorial/profile → owned artifact → named product action. If any part is unavailable, choose a working route and describe it accurately. Keep tutorial viewers, onward clicks and useful actions separate. [YouTube link behavior](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13748639?hl=en)

If founder access or observed audience fit favors Reels instead, substitute it for the short-video workload after verifying its link behavior. [Pinterest and portable templates](18-growth-options.md#route-portfolio) are candidates for a later bounded test, not extra weekly obligations. A long tutorial, a short cut and a saved visual have different discovery and click mechanics.

| Lane | Proposed placement | Reason and constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Builders | One useful DEV Community adaptation; a relevant Indie Hackers feedback discussion or an existing maker-group host. | A project tutorial fits the task. Verify current affiliation/link rules and use a permitted feedback slot; no assumption that a community accepts promotion. |
| Lifters | A coach's opt-in training group; a moderator-approved relevant thread in a lifting community such as r/Fitness. | The artifact explains repeating a routine. Ask the host or moderator which format is welcome before including a product link; do not send unsolicited DMs. |
| Writers | A journaling-group host or a permitted tutorial discussion in r/Journaling. | Teach retrieval or a reflection practice using invented entries. Participation and private writing remain separate. |

Seek **one actual host placement in the first 14-day cycle**, matched to the companion kit. A second contextual placement is optional within the same block; the lanes do not each receive a quota. An external adaptation consumes that block during its pilot week. Check community rules at the time of
placement, disclose authorship and record declines. Reddit prohibits spam and lets communities set their
own rules. [Community practice](15-community-answering.md) ·
[Reddit spam policy](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043504051-Spam)

## External publishing pilot

Select **at most one adaptation in the base 14-day cycle**: Telegraph or an audience-fit channel such as a permitted builder tutorial on DEV Community. The second platform remains a reserve until the first result and time ledger justify it. Verify publishing and affiliation rules before selection. The owned original publishes first. Each adaptation must stand alone as a useful tutorial or excerpt, name Windmill's authorship, link the original and end with one relevant CTA. A source attribution and the CTA may point to the same owned resource; do not scatter keyword links through the text.

Telegraph is a publishing surface to test reader referrals. Its documented API exposes title, authorship and content but no canonical-setting field. Do not assume control over a canonical tag or a ranking benefit. Prefer a distinct useful adaptation or short excerpt there; on another platform, use supported canonical settings only after verifying the rendered result. Canonical signals help search engines choose among duplicates but do not guarantee their selection. [Telegraph](https://telegra.ph/) · [Official introduction](https://telegram.org/blog/instant-view) · [Telegraph API](https://telegra.ph/api) · [Google canonical guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls)

No mass copies, thin AI-generated pages, links bought to manipulate rankings, automated backlink campaigns or reciprocal-link quotas. Legitimate disclosed sponsorships remain a separate owner-approved paid option. Google identifies link manipulation and scaled content created to manipulate rankings as spam. Count helpful referral actions and returns; backlink totals are not the goal. [Google spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)

## Continue, rework and expand

Before each placement, record one named editorial outcome, its observation window and its denominator. Examples are a first Roadmap completion, a first saved Gym set, a first saved Journal page, a successful Journal retrieval or an accurate first assistant read. A save and a retrieval are separate outcomes; select the one demonstrated by that piece. Use distinct attributable visitors or a fixed consented trial group as the denominator, name which it is, and leave a missing denominator unknown.

For each piece or placement record original URL, bounded source, date, actual founder minutes, publication/placement status, attributable visits, people completing that predeclared action, mature returns and unknown attribution. The full Roadmap and Gym cohort activation thresholds remain separate measures with the exact definitions and denominators in the [scorecard](../funnels.md). An editorial first action does not satisfy a full cohort activation gate. Do not merge unlike actions into one conversion rate. Search coverage, impressions, placements and store-page visits diagnose reach; they do not establish product use.

At day 14, continue a format for one more cycle if at least one attributable person completed that placement’s predeclared editorial action within its time allocation, or if its return window is still maturing after that action. With visits but no useful action, inspect the destination and promise before making more copies. With no observed referrals, change the placement or format once; if attribution is missing, repair observation and label the result unknown. This is a small operating rule, not statistical proof. Keep indexing repairs and essential identity work running even when a format is paused. Do not demand search ranking within 14 days.

After launch, invite established users to share voluntarily and measure recipient first actions and returns using [the existing share path](07-sharing-surfaces.md). No Journal content is needed. Paid referrals, masters or ads wait for the owner's spend approval, functioning One offer and [contribution gates](../strategy.md#when-money-enters-the-plan); use [Apple Ads](14-apple-ads-test.md) only when its prerequisites hold. Never replace qualified outcomes with follower, impression or backlink targets.
