# 18 · Make a useful plan travel

**Proposed direction: let a useful plan travel through a trusted host, answer a precise question, and help someone try their next undertaking without moving their history.** Windmill needs evidence of repeated use and a repeatable way to reach people. More channels alone do not supply either. Roadmap is the proposed first demonstration, not an established winning segment; Gym and Journal remain credible entry points to the same app.

This is the strategic research and decision home. Primary sources were checked on **November 17, 2026**. Platform capabilities and competitor offerings below are evidence of what is available, not evidence of Windmill demand, audience access or conversion. All first tests, priorities and targets are proposals. No outreach, publication, account creation, spend or product implementation is recorded by this document. [The launch contract](../strategy.md#launch-contract) remains unchanged: complete three-room iOS scope, with no date and no extra marketing feature required for release.

## Ansoff: separate distribution from new product work

“Existing market” here means our current target segments—builders, routine-following lifters and private writers. It does **not** mean established product-market fit. Classify an idea by what changes for the person, not by the platform carrying the link.

| Quadrant | Windmill application | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Existing product, current segments | Host placement, query tutorials, useful public examples, store intent matching, existing MCP discovery and voluntary referrals. | First priority. Improve delivery of the real product and observe repeat use. A new channel is not automatically a new market. |
| Existing product, new segments | Test a course cohort, hack-day organizer or a new language community whose job fits an existing action. | Bounded market-development tests. Confirm the job and access first; do not infer demand from an organizer directory. |
| New product, current segments | A new importer, interactive calculator, calendar integration, reusable template marketplace or automated recap. | Product-development hypotheses. First test the need with a document or consented manual process. Estimate engineering, privacy and maintenance before requesting code; none becomes a release dependency. |
| New product, new segments | Team learning administration, institutional dashboards, employer wellness, or a marketplace with seller payments. | Defer as diversification. No verified buyer, procurement path or operating capacity supports it today. |

Eight owner hours a week, with spending only by approved proposal, is the operating plan, not a filter that excludes larger options from research. There is no automatic 70/20/10 allocation. Spend most immediate effort on existing capabilities until a specific obstacle justifies a new one.

## Blue Ocean: compete on the effort around a task

The available evidence does not establish an uncontested feature category. A skill tree, AI help, sharing or voice alone is not a verified moat. The following is a qualitative factor comparison; **unknown** is deliberately not a low score.

| Factor a person may value | Evidence from alternatives | Windmill evidence and open question |
|---|---|---|
| Ready-made paths and customization | [roadmap.sh](https://roadmap.sh/premium) offers community/custom roadmaps, AI and team plans. | Ship v1 exists; the question is whether prerequisites help someone execute their own project again. Native Roadmap is a launch dependency. |
| Trusted coach and reusable routine | [Boostcamp](https://www.boostcamp.app/all-coaches) distributes named coaches' programs; [Hevy](https://www.hevyapp.com/features/share-folders-routines/) supports readable shared routines. | Gym logging is a current capability; host interest, recipient setup effort and repeat logging through this channel are unknown. Do not claim invented superiority over these products. |
| Voice capture and reflection | [Day One](https://dayoneapp.com/guides/tips-and-tutorials/audio-recording/) documents audio transcription. | Live Journal Talk is required for launch. Whether retrieval or Talk brings a writer back is a separate observation. |
| Social accountability and game framing | [Habitica](https://habitica.com/static/features) describes group challenges. | Roadmap's animated execution is demonstrable, but no evidence proves greater motivation or retention. Private use must remain a complete path. |
| Low-effort trial without migration | Existing notes, spreadsheets and calendars can remain in place. No measured competitor setup comparison is available. | “Try your next project/session/page” is an offer hypothesis using existing actions. Measure unaided first action, then second use; no import promise. |

**Eliminate:** migration as a prerequisite in the invitation; generic “all-in-one productivity” claims; public sharing as a condition of participation; unmeasured channel quotas. **Reduce:** initial choices, setup explanation, feature comparison breadth and recurring content production. **Raise:** task clarity, readable examples, honest access/allowance explanations, host relevance and evidence of the second use. **Create:** a portable companion kit and a continuous route from useful example to personal execution. The kit is new to Windmill's distribution; it is not claimed to be new to the market.

The buyer-utility opportunity is mainly **discovery, delivery, setup and maintenance**. A host supplies context, a readable artifact supplies value before signup, the next undertaking avoids migration, and a second visit tests whether maintaining progress is worthwhile. Product use still has to succeed; distribution cannot repair a broken first action.

| Noncustomer tier | Concrete person and barrier | Test that stays within existing capability |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Almost using | A builder tries planners but returns to a checklist because maintaining the plan costs too much. | Give them a three-step plan for their next project; observe setup and next use without transferring old projects. |
| 2 · Deliberately refusing | A lifter rejects social feeds or a writer refuses to publish personal reflection. | Offer private logging or a synthetic reflection example. Ask whether the private task is useful; never make sharing the success criterion. |
| 3 · Not considering this category | A course learner or club participant sees the activity as learning/practice, not “using a productivity app.” | Let the host offer an optional companion for that activity. A host can decline; the activity still works without Windmill. |

These are recruiting hypotheses, not descriptions of measured users. Do not infer a person's motives from their segment.

## What stays off the active queue

An affiliate program needs checkout attribution, refunds, payouts, terms and engineering; an App Store offer code alone is not that system. Vendor capabilities such as [Tolt's RevenueCat integration](https://tolt.com/revenuecat-affiliate-program) establish neither a Windmill integration nor viable economics. Consider it only after a repeatable paid channel and an owner-approved build/spend case. A paid listing, an affiliate program and an earned editorial review are different mechanisms. Earned expert demonstrations or podcast guest appearances remain a candidate within the [editorial queue](17-distribution-and-editorial.md#earned-editorial-and-directories); editorial interest is discretionary. [MacStories' sponsorship policy](https://www.macstories.net/macstories-sponsorship-policy/) explicitly separates payment from editorial coverage.

[BetaList](https://betalist.com/help) currently requires payment and does not accept a direct App Store link, so it would need its own spending proposal. [Buildspace](https://buildspace.so/final) is closed, so it is not a current outreach prospect. [TestFlight](https://developer.apple.com/testflight/) is distribution infrastructure with external review, not a built-in audience. [App Store pre-orders](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/pre-orders/) need a date and review; Windmill has no release date. [In-app events](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/in-app-events/) need a genuine time-bounded experience and destination; do not invent one to fill a marketing calendar or add it to release scope.

## Balanced Scorecard: test the causal chain

The hypothesis is **better task/host understanding → a clearer, reliable delivery path → more useful second uses → an economically sustainable service**. Each arrow can fail. Counts below have no verified baseline yet; targets are proposed operating decisions, not forecasts. [Funnels](../funnels.md) owns action definitions and attribution limits. Founder owns the Monday decision; engineering/release owners supply verified evidence.

| Perspective and owner | Metric, denominator and source | Proposed target and review |
|---|---|---|
| Learning · founder | Documented host-fit decisions / host conversations actually completed; private bounded-reason ledger, no participant content. Separately record actual placements / accepted placements. | Resolve two relevant host conversations in the first cycle when outreach is authorized; seek one actual placement. Review at day 14; no agreement means revise the offer, not infer audience demand. |
| Internal process · engineering + founder | Verified readable/actionable destinations / destinations proposed for use; synthetic click-path record. Founder minutes / artifact and placement from time ledger. | Check every destination before use; keep the base cycle within its 8-hour weekly assumption. Review before publishing and Monday. Failed paths block that placement. |
| Customer · founder + analyst | Host recipient own-first-action / observed distinct recipient starters; then second use / same starters with mature windows shown. Use consented observations or verified scoped events. Keep Roadmap, Gym and Journal rows separate. | For editorial delivery, at least one attributable named action by day 14 earns another bounded cycle; later-use window can remain pending. Expansion uses the existing Roadmap 8/20 activation + 4/20 return and Gym 4/10 two-session gates. Review only when their clocks mature. |
| Customer · release owner | CPP views and first-time downloads for the same page/window/storefront, where available; App Store Connect. Separately show product task outcomes and unknown attribution. | First establish a readable baseline and working route; no invented install-conversion target at low volume. | Financial · founder + analyst | Founder hours / observed returning people by source; cash and allocated variable cost by cohort. If there are no returning people or payers, the corresponding cost ratio is undefined; report cost and zero people. Missing store reports, attribution or outcomes are unknown, never zeros. A new event or new UI flow needs its normal engineering/design process; this scorecard does not assert instrumentation exists. Use first-party events with allowlisted bounded labels and static failure names. Keep contact details, private exports, query text, participant plans, workout names, journal content and secrets out of public artifacts and analytics. Only explicitly owner-listed original trees enter public search; an unlisted link is not permission to index it.
