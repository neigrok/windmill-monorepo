# 15 · Recruit through relevant conversations

**Supporting channel for the two pilots.** The founder can use existing relationships, a builder feedback
thread, or a coach willing to pass along an invitation. Count this time within the eight-hour weekly cap for
the whole plan. A few personal conversations can identify the right twenty builders and ten lifters more
directly than an open-ended posting schedule. [Roadmap invitation](11-roadmap-seed-trees.md) ·
[Gym invitation](04-gym-demo-and-checkout.md)

1. Pick a real question about shipping a project or repeating a workout program. Check the community's
   current rules for links, affiliation and recruitment. If a thread is for technical answers only, answer
   the question there and recruit elsewhere. Reddit's policy prohibits repeated or unsolicited mass
   engagement and leaves communities room to set their own rules.
   [Reddit spam policy](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043504051-Spam)
2. Answer the question in a way someone can use without opening Windmill. Where an invitation is welcome,
   disclose that you build the product, name the free task and the supported route. A founder posting in a
   permitted builder feedback thread might write, “I build Windmill. If you're working on a first release, I
   have a free starter tree you can adapt. Would you like to try it on your own project?”
3. Respond personally to people who opt in. Use a bounded source label such as `builder_community`,
   `coach_intro` or `direct`. Keep participant names and message text out of campaign analytics. Do not send
   unsolicited bulk DMs.

**Weekly decision.** Record founder minutes, qualified opt-ins and people who actually try, by source. Later
append cohort activation and mature return where attributable. If a channel spends its allotted time
producing no qualified trials, move the next recruiting block to introductions. If a moderator declines,
stop recruiting there. Replies and upvotes have no value in the demand denominator.
