# 15 · Disclosed community answering

Kind: supporting · Stage: 0 onward · Window: ongoing, capped at 2 CEO hours a week · Cash: $0 · CEO hours / agent days: 2h a week / 1d setup

## Idea

Answer questions lifters and journalers already ask, about routine setup, logging and journaling,
in the places they ask them. Answers are useful on their own, disclose that we make Windmill, use
brand-owned screen recordings where a recording helps, and a named human handles follow-ups. A
Reddit Pro brand profile holds the identity. Participation follows the 90/10 norm: most activity is
not about us.

## Pros

- Intent and feedback at zero cash.
- Good answers are search presence: Reddit appears in 28.7% of AI Overviews.
- The named human is the one place the CEO's voice helps without a face on camera.
- Listening surfaces the words people use, which feeds store copy and pages.

## Cons

- Attention-heavy and easy to overspend time on.
- Subreddit rules are strict: r/Fitness (12.6M members) allows promotion only in its self-promotion
  thread with moderator approval and bans otherwise; r/bodybuilding bans self-promotion accounts.
- Reddit's search visibility has trended down since August 2025.
- Attribution is weak; buyers rarely arrive by the link.

## Success metric

At least 5 buyers within six weeks at or below 1 CEO hour per buyer.

## Failure metric

More than 8 hours spent for fewer than 2 buyers in six weeks. On failure, community work becomes
listening only: read, do not post, and feed what people say into reports 05 and 06.

## Justification

Reddit is a listening channel, not a launch channel; Hevy grew on word of mouth and runs its own
subreddit; no launch post of its own into other subreddits was found. Lifting communities ban promotion, so the only honest
posture is answering well and saying who we are. This stays a supporting action below ASO and the
search identity work because its labour economics are poor until a buyer costs less than an hour.
Manufactured endorsements, undisclosed accounts and upvote buying are excluded by the brand canon
and by the subreddits' rules.

## Dependencies

- Live subreddit rules verified before the first post; mirrors are stale.
- Report 06's brand profile names and pages, so answers link somewhere that resolves.
- Screen recordings show shipped behaviour only.
