# 09 · Ask one master for their own opinion after purchase and retention are observable

**Deferred channel option.** The current pilots spend nothing. A paid placement needs an open
experiment slot, a verified destination accessible to the master's audience, a fee the owner has approved
as its own proposal, a working paid offer and evidence that lifters return. A beta installation rate observed with
founder help provides a poor forecast for a public campaign. [Gym repeat-use test](08-gym-demand-cohort.md) ·
[Paid-pilot prerequisites](04-gym-demo-and-checkout.md)

**Who a master is.** A recognised practitioner with a good reputation in their craft, whose audience
follows their work and who says what they think. Start with a lifting practitioner who shows a repeat
program, for Gym. An engineer for Roadmap is a second door, opened only once Roadmap's own cohort shows
returning use. [Roadmap cohort](11-roadmap-seed-trees.md)

**The rules**

1. They use the actual product first, on their own work, before anything is agreed about publishing.
2. A fee is allowed. It is flat, agreed in writing and disclosed in the piece, including in a video. There
   is no pay per view, per install or per sale.
   [FTC disclosure guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers)
3. There is no script. Nothing Windmill supplies is presented as theirs. Windmill may check product claims
   for accuracy; the opinion and the words are the master's.
4. "I did not like it" and silence are allowed outcomes. The fee never depends on what they say. It buys
   their time, never their verdict.

Before a master starts, agree in writing on the trial period, the flat fee, whether it is owed when
nothing is published, the disclosure, whether Windmill may reuse the piece, maximum liability and the reporting window. An unpaid opinion needs no agreement and is theirs to give or
withhold. [Earned editorial](17-distribution-and-editorial.md#earned-editorial-and-directories)

**If they publish.** Give the master a dedicated source link. Before publication, check the journey through
install, first log and payment in the appropriate environment. At readout, show link visits, installs where
attributable, seven-day qualified users, settled first purchases and their observation windows. Preserve
unassigned installs. Treat reported views as reach. Track fee, founder hours at the agreed internal rate
and other acquisition costs.

**Cash decision.** Start with one master. There is no standing ceiling: each fee goes to the owner as its
own proposal, with its amount and what it would teach. No fee benchmark is recorded in
[the evidence](../benchmarks.md), so the fee is unknown until a real quote. Fix the acceptable learning
loss, exposure window and mature payer denominator before agreeing. With zero attributable payers, CAC
cannot be estimated. With few payers, report the observed count and cost without extrapolating. A second
master is a new proposal, made only against the first one's evidence and to answer a stated question the
first left open. Scaling
requires successive cohorts whose fully loaded CAC fits realized 90-day contribution per acquired payer
(C90). [Economics and investment gates](../strategy.md#when-money-enters-the-plan)
