# 09 · Test one lifting creator after purchase and retention are observable

**Deferred channel option.** The current pilots have $0 incremental spend. A paid creator test needs an open
experiment slot, a verified destination accessible to the advertised audience, an owner-approved spending
cap, a working paid offer and evidence that lifters return. A beta installation rate observed with founder
help provides a poor forecast for a public campaign. [Gym repeat-use test](08-gym-demand-cohort.md) ·
[Paid-pilot prerequisites](04-gym-demo-and-checkout.md)

**Creator brief.** Choose someone who shows a repeat program and whose audience follows it. Ask them to try
the actual product, demonstrate one workout and revisit its history during the next. Agree in writing on
deliverable, publication date, paid relationship, reuse rights, fee or payout floor, maximum liability and
reporting window. Keep disclosure clear in the endorsement, including in the video. Review for accurate
product claims while preserving the creator's own experience.
[FTC disclosure guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers)

Give the creator a dedicated source link. Before publication, check the journey through install, first log
and payment in the appropriate environment. At readout, show link visits, installs where attributable,
seven-day qualified users, settled first purchases and their observation windows. Preserve unassigned
installs. Treat creator-reported views as reach. Track fee, production, founder hours at the agreed internal
rate and other acquisition costs.

**Cash decision.** Start with one bounded placement. Fix the acceptable learning loss, exposure window and
mature payer denominator before contracting. Stop at the cap. With zero attributable payers, CAC cannot be
estimated. With few payers, report the observed count and cost without extrapolating. Replicate only within
the owner's approved budget and to answer a stated question the first placement left open. Scaling requires
successive cohorts whose fully loaded CAC fits realized 90-day contribution per acquired payer (C90).
