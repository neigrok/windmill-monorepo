# 09 · The staged creator campaign

Kind: experiment · Stage: 1 · Window: recruit from Nov 30, 2026; preflight in December; spend Dec 26, 2026 to Jan 15, 2027; read Feb 19, 2027 · Cash: $1,500 to $3,000 maximum · CEO hours / agent days: 8h / 4d

## Idea

Fifteen to twenty-five US lifting micro-creators (10K to 100K followers) make disclosed,
product-led demonstrations of Gym and Coach, with brand-supplied vignettes allowed. Each creator
gets an owned redirect link and a custom product page. Pay is per qualified view at $1 to $2 per
1,000, with a $50 to $150 floor inside the capped payout, not added to it. Creators are contracted
in batches of five and cash is released in three tranches against gates on what we buy: installs,
activated lifters and payers.

## Pros

- The one acquisition channel whose economics can fit a 2% to 3% freemium conversion.
- Faceless, brand-led content matches the stance in strategy.md.
- Spend lands in Q5, when Health & Fitness downloads run above the yearly average.
- Every creator link carries attribution from the store page onward.
- Demonstrations are reusable as brand assets with usage rights.

## Cons

- Floors consume cash before a single view: twenty $100 floors are $2,000.
- Micro-creator reach is uneven and audience fit is hard to judge before posting.
- Attribution loses installs that bypass the link.
- Operations get chaotic past ten creators without tooling.
- Two tranches may pass on reach and still fail on trust.

Tranches, cumulative:

| Tranche | Cumulative | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | $500 | preflight passed: stores live, checkout working, task costs measured, free demonstrations funded, five genuine buyers with repeat AI use |
| 2 | $1,500 | at least 100 attributed installs, 3 full-price purchases, 20% D7 workout activation (two sessions of at least five sets within 7 days), 10% applied-Coach activation |
| 3 | $3,000 | tranche-2 thresholds met independently by at least two creators, cost per D7-activated user at or below $5, projected fully loaded CAC at or below 2 × conservatively forecast C90 |

## Success metric

Learning pass: at least 1,000 installs and at least 20 day-35 buyers by Feb 19, 2027. Profitability
is a separate gate (Stage 2 in strategy.md).

## Failure metric

Under 1% paid after 1,000 mature installs, or any tranche gate missed. On failure, spend less and
keep the balance. If two tranches fail on trust rather than reach, test a founder voice-over format
with no face before any spend increase.

## Justification

Paid installs do not pay back at median freemium conversion, so the test has to be the channel
where founders report sub-dollar installs. Cal AI reports $0.50 to $3 per 1,000 views with about
ten installs per 1,000 views early, and Rizz reports about $2M paid to faceless creators at $1 to
$2 per 1,000 views; both are founder claims and justify an experiment, never a budget. A former Hevy marketer
reports a forty-creator ambassador programme there (self-reported). Three flat-fee creators at $150 to $350
would be a demonstration, not a test, and Meta or TikTok ads at $2 to $12 per install cannot be
read below $500 a week per ad set. Install volume alone does not justify the spend; the tranches
exist so cash follows evidence.

## Dependencies

- Reports 01, 02, 03 and 04 complete; report 05's custom product pages live.
- Creator brief vignettes designed and approved in Figma before an agent produces them.
- Contract terms fixed before signing: payout floors, qualified-view definition, measurement
  window, usage rights, maximum liability.
- CEO recruits and briefs creators; agents build links, pages and the weekly read.
