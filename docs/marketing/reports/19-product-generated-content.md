# 19 · Turn an action into something worth keeping

**Generate a small useful or playful artifact from a real action, then help the person take their next real action.** Windmill can be worth returning to because it remembers the work and makes progress legible. A funny receipt can add delight; it cannot substitute for a useful plan, a trustworthy log or a private journal.

This is a product-content assessment, not approved product scope. Sources and current contracts were reviewed on **November 17, 2026**. No code, interface, real-user processing, publication or new release dependency is authorized here. [The launch contract](../strategy.md#launch-contract) is unchanged. The [growth options](18-growth-options.md) provide the Ansoff, Blue Ocean and Balanced Scorecard context: a public artifact can test distribution, while a new personalized generator is product development and must earn its engineering cost.

“Addictive” is translated here into **voluntary second use, a useful next task and delight**. Time in a feed, regeneration loops, pressure to preserve a streak and compulsive checking are not success measures. The default should let someone finish, keep what helped, and leave.

## The three bets, in order

| Bet | Useful core and optional delight | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Roadmap next action and chapter receipt | A grounded next available step plus a short receipt of what actually changed. A light caption can turn the receipt into a memorable project story. | Start here, manually on synthetic fixtures, then only inside the next unfrozen Roadmap intake. It can reuse the companion-kit distribution test. Do not add another cohort. |
| 2 · Gym next-session brief and playful receipt | On request, summarize the previous relevant session and what is already planned. A separate optional caption can make the completed-session receipt enjoyable. | Candidate after the first bet is read or a Gym slot is deliberately used. Coach does not initiate a check-in. No new training recommendation, automatic routine change or unsolicited Coach message. |
| 3 · Journal grounded time capsule and optional question | Retrieve the person's exact words with a date/source and offer one skippable, non-interpretive invitation to add context. | Private candidate after a slot opens. Preserve Echoes' quotation contract. No personality diagnosis, fabricated memory or social-sharing loop. |

Start only one. The Roadmap lead is a testable priority, not proof that its audience or humor will outperform Gym or Journal. Stronger comic or roast styles are research options requiring an owner tone ruling before design/build; current canon is encouraging and a little playful, **not jokey**. ## Twelve concrete content concepts

All examples below are **synthetic fixtures, not customer data**. Priority means research order. “Template” means deterministic facts with authored wording; “bounded LLM” means facts are supplied and checked outside the model. Cost bands are relative planning judgments: low = no model call; variable = a metered text call; high = new integration/media or substantial review. They are not price estimates.

|---|---|---|---|
| 01 · Public witty quest generator · candidate acquisition | A visitor supplies a non-sensitive goal and chooses a tone. | 11 · Requested Windmill week · research only | Person explicitly selects supported Roadmap and Gym facts and period. Example: “Two project steps completed. The public generator, quest cards and host pack should be useful without private history. Personalized outputs stay private unless the product already permits a separate, explicit, previewed share path. Sanitization is a user-reviewed operation, not a promise that a model can safely remove every personal detail. No public artifact needs an account identifier, contact detail, private URL, journal entry or workout name.

## A generation contract worth testing

1. **Ask for scope.** Start from one selected task, record or period, with an explicit request where the product requires it. Never infer permission to read another room. Private fixture evaluation precedes any real-user processing.
2. **Compute facts outside the model.** Resolve identities, time windows, completion state, undo/deletion, session counts and available steps through the domain. Missing data remains missing. The generator cannot write logs, mark tasks complete or change routines.
3. **Select one useful event.** Prefer the next action, a true milestone or a passage the person chose. If there is insufficient information, return a plain factual artifact or nothing extra; never invent a story to fill space.
4. **Choose the cheapest sufficient expression.** Start with authored text and reusable layouts. A model may vary a bounded caption while preserving source facts. Generate once on request and reuse appropriately; no automatic video, endless regeneration or background narrative stream.
5. **Validate and keep provenance.** Compare every number, identity and quotation with source facts. Keep source navigation and the selected scope visible. If an underlying fact changes, revalidate or retire the derived content. Do not treat arbitrary journal text as instructions.
6. **Let the person decide.** Plain mode remains complete. Optional playful output can be edited, rejected or disabled. A shareable product shows a separate preview and explicit share action; Journal remains private. Dismissal must not block the next manual action.

This is a proposed contract, not a claim that the validators, UI or generated-artifact storage exist. New code must follow the existing domain/action boundaries and approved Figma flow. Text/visual templates are the first prototype because they let us test the content value without a media pipeline.

## Quality gate and bounded experiment

Before showing a prototype to participants, run **20 synthetic cases**: two variations for each row below. Store the fixture, expected facts and reviewed result locally for the test; no customer data is needed. A reviewer who did not author the captions checks the whole set. This gate has not been run by this strategy task.

| Two-case category | Required result |
|---|---|
| Empty history / one valid record | No invented progress or forced generation; a plain useful fallback. |
| Deleted step / undone completion | Derived counts and next action reflect current facts. |
| Duplicate workout / unfinished workout | No double-counting or claim that an open session was finished. |
| Time-zone boundary / partial selected period | Correct dates and explicit scope; no assumed missing-day failure. |
| Missing load / missing previous session | No fabricated comparison, effort estimate or advice. |
| Edited quotation / deleted quotation | Exact source revalidation; retire an invalid capsule. |
| Private URL in selected text / third person's name | Keep out of public fixtures and share output; require review, never claim automatic anonymization. |
| Instruction-like text / request to invent an achievement | Treat source text as data; refuse factual invention and preserve the useful manual path. |
| Bereavement-like fixture / injury-like fixture | No comedy, roast, diagnosis, interpretation or unsolicited advice; plain factual handling or no artifact. |
| Disabled playful mode / denied extra-room scope | No humorous output or extra-room access; no retry pressure. |

Required gate: every factual, quotation, scope and privacy case passes; subjective amusement can vary. A wrong fact or privacy leak stops participant exposure until fixed and rerun. Tone approval does not waive factual correctness.

Then propose a small manual plain-versus-playful evaluation inside the next **unfrozen** Roadmap intake, using at most five opted-in participants from that same cohort. Show matched synthetic examples in alternating order for preference, then offer only the predeclared content variant for the participant's task. This is formative feedback, not an efficacy A/B test. Do not alter the frozen starter destination or the formal 20-person denominator/gates. If no slot or follow-up capacity exists, defer it. Gym and Journal tests wait their turn.

Record the count of people who find the artifact useful, amusing, unwanted/creepy or factually wrong; permit “none.” Keep those labels separate. Record whether they accept/keep it, take the named next action, and return in the existing cohort window. A screenshot, share, regeneration or laugh is not activation. Raw plans, captions, writing and transcript text stay out of marketing analytics. No testimonials or public examples are taken from private feedback without separate explicit permission.

Proposed continue rule: all factual/privacy gates pass; at least three of the five can use the artifact without help; at least one performs its predeclared next action; and the time/cost fits the allocated cycle. That earns another small test, not scale. Any wrong fact/privacy failure pauses the prototype. If playful content is amusing but less useful or unwanted, keep the plain form and revise or stop the joke. A return window that has not matured remains pending; the existing Roadmap/Gym expansion gates do not change.

## Measure value, burden and cost

The [Balanced Scorecard](18-growth-options.md#balanced-scorecard-test-the-causal-chain) remains the strategic scorecard. For this bet, learning is whether the artifact helps and which tone is welcome; process is factual correctness and effort; customer value is the next task and second use; financial value is the cost of that useful behavior.

| Readout | Denominator and evidence |
|---|---|
| Useful / amused / unwanted / wrong fact | People shown the artifact in the declared test, with unknowns shown. Bounded voluntary labels; no combined “delight score.” |
| Accepted artifact | Distinct people keeping/accepting one output / people offered it. Previewing, regenerating and sharing are separate events. |
| Next action and second use | Same distinct starters, the declared action/window and the existing cohort definition. Keep assisted use and mature windows visible. |
| Cost per accepted artifact | Actual model, retry and rendering cost plus allocated review time / accepted artifacts. If none are accepted, report cost and zero accepted; ratio undefined. |
| Cost per useful next action | Same test's total cost / verified named next actions; do not substitute model calls, views or shares as the denominator. |
| Failure and rejection | Technical failures, expected refusals, invalid facts, rejected tone and unknown outcomes separately. Future code uses static failure names and allowlisted first-party events; no input/output text in telemetry. |

No baseline, event implementation, cost advantage or retention uplift is claimed. A future generator needs an explicit allowance/cost contract and surface observability documentation before shipping. Signed-out manual use continues regardless of whether generation is available.
