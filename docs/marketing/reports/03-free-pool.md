# 03 · Keep free AI demonstrations within existing funding

**Operating check for the two free pilots.** Manual tree editing and workout logging carry both hypotheses.
Optional AI use draws on existing funded capacity. The plan allocates $0 incremental cash.

| Production policy | Available capacity |
|---|---|
| Anonymous Gym onboarding | $0.25 over the installation credential's lifetime |
| Verified free account | $0.50 per UTC calendar month, including applicable current-month anonymous spend |
| Shared free pool | $5 per UTC day and $100 per UTC month |
| Funded One period | $7 for its billing period, isolated from the free pool |

Roadmap Tend uses the applicable USD allowance. Journal Echoes debits the same individual allowance,
including automatic background work. Talk is refused while audio bounds and pricing remain unresolved.
Ordinary Roadmap or Gym MCP actions performed by an external assistant incur no Windmill-hosted model cost.
Grants determine which products and levels it can access. [AI usage policy](../../AI_USAGE_REVIEW.md) ·
[MCP product boundary](../../../STRUCTURE.md)

**Before a Coach demonstration**, check current capacity, any unsettled holds and whether the build supports
the proposed task. Show manual setup if a request is refused. In the pilot read, count attempts, successful
jobs, refusal category and users who continued manually. Keep the prompt and program private. Check the
existing owner alerts at 50%, 80% and 95% occupancy when available.
[Allowance accounting](../../../backend/AI_ALLOWANCES.md)

**Before any expansion**, estimate additional cost as expected tasks by operation × observed mean settled
cost per task, including charged failures. Inspect peak-day demand and unsettled holds separately. Where
costs or observations are missing, leave the reserve unpriced. Bring measured demand, the proposed cap and a
pause rule to the owner before raising either budget or offering grants.
