# 13 · The Journal voice cohort

Kind: experiment · Stage: 1 · Window: recruit Jan 16 to 31, 2027; read Mar 2, 2027 · Cash: $0 to $50 · CEO hours / agent days: 5h / 1d

## Idea

Recruit forty people who already journal by voice and offer them the actual Windmill One at $12 a
month, with Voice transcription as the only paid behaviour. The cohort tests whether Journal's
shipped paid behaviour earns the price on its own. Echoes are free and stay out of the read.

## Pros

- Tests the real paid behaviour, not a promised one.
- Daily writing can produce recurring demand where setup-only jobs cannot.
- Cheap: a recruiting list, a disclosed offer, the funnel views.
- Settles whether Journal earns acquisition attention before any is spent.

## Cons

- Weak differentiation at $12 against apps that bundle AI reflection.
- Apple Journal is free and built in on iOS.
- Privacy expectations around audio are high.
- Forty hand-picked journalers inflate conversion.

## Estimates

| Item | Number | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Cash | $0 to $50 | transcription cost on the allowance |
| Effort | 5 CEO hours, 1 agent day | recruiting and the read |
| Buyers | 0 to 4 | assumption |
| Comparables | Rosebud $12.99 a month or $107.99 a year, no free plan since Sep 2026; Day One Gold $74.99 a year; Stoic AI $99.99 a year | 2026 pricing |

## Success metric

At least 4 buyers and at least 50% of buyers use Voice again in another week, by Mar 2, 2027.

## Failure metric

Fewer than 2 buyers, or under 30% repeat use. On failure Journal stays retention and featuring
material and receives no acquisition spend until more paid behaviour ships. Echoes engagement
cannot rescue the paid test.

## Justification

The journaling category has repriced upward and proves people pay for AI journaling, which is why
Journal gets a cohort at all. It is not the wedge because transcription is the only behaviour that
draws the allowance, and the comparables sell reflection, summaries and chat. Stoic and Finch grew
for years on zero paid spend and Apple featuring, so the sequence for Journal is featuring and word
of mouth first. The cohort gives a yes or no on the paid behaviour before any channel is pointed at
it.

## Dependencies

- Voice shipped on all three platforms, or the cohort runs on the platforms where it is.
- Report 02's allowance ledger and report 04's checkout.
- CEO recruits the forty journalers.
