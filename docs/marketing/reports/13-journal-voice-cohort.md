# 13 · Learn whether writers return to a manual Journal page

**Deferred manual-retention experiment.** Journal is on the web and in the iOS TestFlight app. When one of the two initial
pilot slots becomes free and a Journal question merits the slot, ask whether someone who already writes will choose to
write again. Manual writing is a job that can be tested now. Talk requires Windmill One, which is not on
sale. The current accounting contract describes automatic Echoes processing; the release moves it onto the new service.
Live Journal Talk is required by the [launch contract](../strategy.md#launch-contract), independently of this
research slot. Journal tutorials and store assets proceed under [the editorial plan](17-distribution-and-editorial.md).
[Journal product role](../../PRODUCT_LOG.md#journal) · [Metered operations](../../AI_USAGE_REVIEW.md)

**Proposed protocol.** Recruit people who already write several days a week and agree to try the supported
web or iOS version privately. The founder checks that a sample page saves and reopens on the offered build,
then gives participants the route. Ask participants to write when they normally would. Explain that they can
report whether they wrote without showing their pages.

Before recruiting, record the cohort size, the return window, the smallest repeat count worth pursuing and
the rule for an inconclusive result. A simple primary measure is the number of enrolled writers who save a
manual page on two distinct local dates within seven days of the first save. Report first saves and
nonstarters separately, keeping all enrolled writers in the denominator. Where no verified cohort view
exists, use voluntary confirmation and label its source.

**Readout.** Ask returners what brought them back and others where they wrote instead. Keep the answers
about the routine. Collect neither excerpts nor screenshots, audio, moods or Echoes matches. A meaningful
manual return supports a further retention test. Voice demand needs its own test once Windmill One, which
carries Talk, is on sale. Paying demand requires a separately approved, functioning paid offer.
