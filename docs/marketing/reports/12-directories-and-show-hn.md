# 12 · Directories and one Show HN

Kind: supporting, one launch · Stage: 0 · Window: submissions by Nov 30, 2026; Show HN Dec 8, 2026; read Jan 7, 2027 · Cash: $0 to $100 · CEO hours / agent days: 3h / 4d

## Idea

Submit complete connector packages for Windmill's MCP server to the Claude Connectors Directory,
the ChatGPT apps directory, Cursor and Smithery by Nov 30, 2026. Then one Show HN on Dec 8, 2026,
a Tuesday to Thursday morning ET, for the working Roadmap WebGL tree with the MCP angle: an agent
builds a tree, a person forks it. Product Hunt is optional reuse of the same package, capped at two
CEO hours. No paid launch packages, no repeated launch campaigns.

## Pros

- Concentrated discovery among people who already use agents.
- The Claude directory is self-serve, usage-ranked and suggested inside chat.
- Show HN rewards craft, and the hand-rolled renderer is the craft story.
- The package is produced once and reused.

## Cons

- Directory review, authentication and verification take time; approval timing is outside our control.
- The ChatGPT apps directory excludes the EU, UK and Switzerland at launch.
- Agent-built trees run on external inference and create no One demand by themselves.
- A launch is a spike, not a channel.

## Success metric

At least 50 activated newcomers and at least 10 returning users by Jan 7, 2027, with resulting Tend
purchases tracked separately.

## Failure metric

Fewer than 20 activations from at least 300 qualified visits. On failure, the listings stay up as
plumbing and no further launch work is scheduled. Directory approval alone is not success.

## Justification

Windmill speaks MCP and nobody else in planning tools has a moving skill tree, so the audience that
uses agents is the one place a launch post has a real story. Listings are cheap once the review
materials exist, but "thirty minutes each" is not reliable: OpenAI requires review materials and
test cases. The evidence for agent-discovered users converting better is a single B2B claim, so the
channel is treated as prosumer discovery until a consumer case appears. Product Hunt's featured
slots fell from 47 to 16 a day and its signup ranges stay out of the forecast, which is why it is
optional reuse and not a second launch.

- Report 02's attribution, so directory and Show HN visits are distinguishable.
