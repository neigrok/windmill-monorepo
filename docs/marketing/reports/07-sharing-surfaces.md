# 07 · Gym and Roadmap sharing surfaces

Kind: build, then experiment · Stage: 0 to 1 · Window: ship by Dec 15, 2026; read by Feb 19, 2027 · Cash: $0 to $100 · CEO hours / agent days: 2h / 4d

## Idea
Build the product-led loop before the social account. Two deliverables: a Gym workout receipt share
card and a Roadmap tree share image, both in the brand's hand-drawn style with no faces, brand-tagged,
behind a user-controlled preview with optional publication, and each carrying a deep link with
attribution. Roadmap already has unlock-video unfurls, fork attribution and progress images; the Gym
card is new. Journal stays private and gets no card.

## Pros
- The cheapest compounding distribution available, and it matches a brand-led stance.
- Shares carry attribution, so referred visits, installs and activations are measurable.
- Reuses Roadmap's shipped loop rather than building a second one.
- A card people choose to post is honest marketing by definition.

## Cons
- A loop needs active users first; it complements acquisition and cannot replace it.
- Attribution is lost when a card is screenshotted instead of linked.
- Low volume until the cohorts and the Q5 campaign land.
- The cards are visual work and wait on approved Figma designs.

| Item | Estimate |

| Effort | 2 CEO hours reviewing designs; 4 agent days for rendering, previews and deep links |
| Evidence | Hevy reports that share-to-Stories cards raised installs 12% and shares 42% in eight weeks; this is Hevy's own claim without a published control, so it justifies the build and no forecast |
| Volume | assumption: 200 eligible active users by Feb 19, 2027 |

## Success metric
By Feb 19, 2027, at least 5% of 200 eligible active users share, and at least 10 referred visitors
activate in their product.

## Failure metric
Under 2% share, or fewer than 3 referred activations, by Feb 19, 2027. The surfaces stay but get no
further work until a cohort is large enough to read.

## Justification
Hevy reached two million downloads on about $15,000 of lifetime ad spend and credits word of mouth
plus its in-app follow graph (RevenueCat podcast, 2024). Good referral programs reach 20% to 30% of
an app's acquisition mix (Adapty, 2026). Those numbers are other companies' and justify an
experiment, not a budget; Windmill's own share-to-activation rate is what the decision log records.
Shares are not subscribers: the card's job is to bring a person to the free product, and contribution
is assessed later through the normal funnel. The alternative, a brand social account posting the
same images, was ranked lower because the evidence for faceless brand accounts driving installs is
weak, and a card posted by a user carries the trust an account cannot.

## Dependencies
- Both cards approved in Figma before an agent implements them (the Gym file and the Roadmap file).
- Report 02's attribution join to read referred visits and activations.
- Report 01's store listings as the deep-link destination on phones.
