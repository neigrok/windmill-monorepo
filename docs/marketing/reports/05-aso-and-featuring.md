# 05 · Make the three-room iPhone app understandable and findable

**Prepare the iPhone listing against the [launch contract](../strategy.md#launch-contract).** Use a broad
Windmill identity for Roadmap, Journal and Gym, with the builder’s job as the proposed lead demonstration, subject to evidence from the room-specific lanes. Try “Pick up your project where you left off” as the headline. Mark a step complete in a
sample project, reopen the tree and show that the same step is still marked complete. Use the actual
native screens and verbs once the candidate supports the sequence. [Public release checks](01-store-readiness.md)

The founder owns a listing worksheet: app name, subtitle, keywords, description, category, screenshots,
preview, storefront and build evidence. Keep Windmill as the umbrella; validate descriptive candidate terms
such as skill tree planner, workout log and private journal against the demonstrated jobs and Apple's
current field rules. Record the chosen wording and observed search terms, not guessed volume or ranking
promises. Read the default listing as a builder, lifter and writer before submission.
[Apple product page](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page/) ·
[App Store search](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/)

Audience-specific pages earn their work when a real distribution lane needs different screenshots and a distinct destination. Keep the default three-room page. Prepare at most one or two variants initially; the release owner checks that every asset matches the candidate. Publication, unique URL and results remain distinct from drafts.

If public review runs long, use “Try Ship v1 on the web” for builders. The release waits for its required scope; only the owner can change that scope. Beta copy names only
the actions in that beta. Android Gym keeps its signed APK route and does not gate iOS.

**First week after release.** Record product-page views and first-time downloads by storefront, source
and reporting window where available. Read those alongside first Roadmap actions and later returns.
Keep unattributed activity visible and prelaunch pilot counts separate. At low volume, investigate
broken routes or misleading screenshots before scheduling a split test. New in-app prompts need an
approved design and an available experiment slot. [Funnel definitions](../funnels.md)

## Match store intent to a room

Apple currently supports up to 70 Custom Product Pages, unique URLs and keyword assignments that can route eligible App Store searches to a selected page. That capacity is not a recommendation to create 70 pages. Start with the strongest verified lane and at most one additional contrasting job. Optional CPP deep links are supported on iOS 18 and later; verify app support and the resulting route before using one. Deep-link implementation is separate work, not a new launch requirement. [Apple Custom Product Pages](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/custom-product-pages/)

| Intent hypothesis | Proposed page evidence and destination | Decision before publication |
|---|---|---|
| Project execution: “skill tree planner” or “project roadmap” | A real native plan, completed step and persistent progress; Roadmap page. | Native Roadmap and advertised action verified on the candidate. A course/club companion lane supplies a reason for this page; the keyword is a hypothesis, not measured volume. |
| Repeat a workout: “workout log” or “routine tracker” | A real routine, saved set and previous-session history; Gym page. | Checked phone flow and accurate free/paid distinction; no unsupported training-outcome promise. |
| Save and find a thought: “private journal”; voice intent only after readiness | A synthetic saved page and retrieval; Talk only when live transcription and access/allowance work. | Do not use a voice screenshot or keyword promise to sell an unavailable function. Keep writing, retrieval and Talk outcomes separate. |

The founder's keyword worksheet records job, candidate phrase, language/storefront, evidence source, page, eligible field, build verification and observed terms when available. Verify current App Store field and keyword-assignment rules in App Store Connect. No invented search volume, competitor ranking score or guaranteed uplift. Do not put another brand's name into metadata merely to intercept it. [App Store search guidance](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/)

One translated listing is a market-development test, not proof that the app supports that language. Choose a language from actual inbound demand and the team's ability to support it; verify the binary, screenshots and help separately before promising localization. [Apple localization](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/localize-app-information/)

## Read results without inventing attribution

Use a different bounded campaign label or page URL for each authorized placement where the platform supports it. Apple campaign links become available after app analytics data exists, and campaign reporting requires at least five first-time downloads. Eligibility and privacy thresholds can leave a report unavailable. Record **unknown**, never zero, and do not claim that a unique link provides a guaranteed person-level web-to-install-to-action join. Keep store views/downloads, observable first tasks and mature returns in separate columns with their own denominator, dates and storefront. [Apple campaign links](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect-analytics/acquisition/campaign-links)

At low volume, prefer a route check and consented observation to an underpowered page contest. When an interpretation depends on attribution that is unavailable, leave it pending. [Scorecard](../funnels.md#public-ios-launch)

After meaningful engagement, Apple's neutral system review request is a possible postlaunch route. Never reward a review, ask only satisfied people, or gate progress on a rating. Verify existing implementation first; a new prompt or trigger needs the owner's approved Figma flow and an available experiment slot. Aggregate reviews cannot be joined back to individual task attempts. [Apple ratings and reviews](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/ratings-and-reviews/)
