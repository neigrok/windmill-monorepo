# Marketing strategy

Agreed on 2026-11-02 between Claude (Fable 5.1) and Codex (GPT-6 Astra, extra-high effort) over
three negotiation rounds, grounded in [benchmarks.md](benchmarks.md). The CEO executes; agents
produce. Each action has its own report in [reports/](reports/) with idea, pros, cons, estimates,
success and failure metrics and justification. [funnels.md](funnels.md) holds the paths and the
instrumentation. Dates after December 2026 are 2027.

## The thesis

Paying subscribers come from one place: Windmill-funded AI that does work a person wants done again.
Windmill One is $12 a month for that AI; everything by hand stays free, signed out included.

- **Gym Coach is the first paid-demand test.** A lifter with a program has a concrete job
  (screenshot or conversation to a routine, changes proposed and applied) and a reason to come back
  for it. Comparable AI-coach apps charge $12.99 to $15.99 a month; log-only apps charge $2.99 to
  $4.99. We sell against the coach band and give the log away.
- **Roadmap is the cheapest discovery.** Its share loop is already built (unlock video unfurls, fork
  attribution, gallery, progress images) and never launched. Trees built by external agents through
  MCP cost Windmill nothing and earn nothing; the paid trigger is Tend, 30 requests free and 300 on
  One.
- **Journal is retention and featuring material.** Voice transcription is its only paid behaviour
  today. Echoes are free. Journaling apps that grew on zero paid spend did it through Apple
  featuring and years of word of mouth.
- **The superapp is a second-order argument.** One job per landing. One account and one allowance
  become a value argument after the first product has earned trust.

## What was rejected

- **Roadmap as the paid wedge.** The loop is real, but forks and MCP-built trees do not touch the
  allowance, and Tend's interface is being redesigned.
- **Journal as the paid wedge.** Rosebud, Day One Gold and Stoic AI prove people pay $75 to $108 a
  year for AI journaling, but with Voice as the only paid behaviour Windmill cannot carry $12 there
  yet.
- **Marketing the superapp first.** Nobody installs three tools for one job.
- **Paid installs as the engine.** Median freemium conversion is 2.1% at day 35 and Apple Ads
  installs cost $3.77 in US Health & Fitness, so a paid install costs $105 to $180 per subscriber
  against roughly $24 of year-one revenue per payer. Paid search is a gated test, not a channel.
- **Launch calendars.** Product Hunt's featured slots fell from 47 to 16 a day and a top-three
  launch is worth 100 to 400 signups. One Show HN, no treadmill.

## Non-negotiables

The brand canon applies to every channel: no urgency, no countdowns, no fake scarcity, no forced
login, no blurred previews or "upgrade to unlock", no number we do not have, no fabricated
testimonials, landings carry no price numbers, copy describes only shipped behaviour. Creator and
UGC content is disclosed and shows the shipped product. Comparison pages describe competitors
accurately. Seed content is original or explicitly licensed. Email carries activity counts and links,
never journal text or workout names. In the UK (DMCCA, fines up to 10% of turnover, countdown-timer
cases since November 2025) and the EU (Digital Fairness Act proposal due Q4 2026) this is also a
compliance advantage; say it plainly in copy.

**Brand-led, founder optional.** All creative is product-as-content under the brand. The CEO appears
as a named human only in community answers, creator relationships and support replies. Revisit
trigger: if two creator tranches fail on trust ("who makes this?") rather than on reach, test a
founder voice-over format with no face before any spend increase.

**Market.** English-first storefronts (US, UK, CA, AU). Creator acquisition starts in the US only,
so a small sample is not split across countries.

**Visual work.** Store screenshots, share cards, custom product pages, the recap email and every
landing change are designed and approved in Figma before an agent implements them.

## Unit economics

| Line | Number |
|---|---|
| One price | $12 a month, one allowance shared across products |
| After Apple's 15% small-business fee | $10.20 (web checkout through Paddle keeps more) |
| Allowance on One | $7 a month; full use leaves $3.20 before any other cost |
| Free AI per verified account | $0.50 a month; $0.25 lifetime per anonymous installation |
| Free pool today | $100 a month and $5 a day shared by every free user: it saturates at 200 fully active verified accounts, so a launch of 2,000 installs would go dark mid-month. Report 03 replaces it |
| Stress case | 49 free active accounts per payer at full use cost $24.50 per payer per month |

**C90** is the realised 90-day contribution per acquired payer after store or processor fees, taxes,
refunds, paid inference, the free and welcome usage attributable to that cohort, and variable support
and storage. **Fully loaded CAC** includes creator management and content production at a $100 per
hour shadow price for CEO time. Scale only when fully loaded CAC is at or below C90. An illustrative
$6.50 monthly contribution with 70% month-two and 55% month-three retention gives a C90 of $14.63,
which is why a $40 CAC that looks cheap can still fail.

Nobody has measured a Coach task's cost yet. The first week of the pilot produces the distribution
of cost per Coach task and per Tend request, and the welcome allowance and the free pool are sized
from arithmetic means, not medians.

## Pricing and checkout decisions

- Open checkout on the web for a controlled pilot by **November 23, 2026**, native purchases working
  at the store releases. Marketing needs a purchasable product; the pilot proves purchase, restore,
  cross-platform entitlement, cancellation and allowance accounting on twenty scenarios first.
- **$12 monthly only** at launch. No lifetime AI, no card-based trial.
- **Welcome allowance**: a one-time $1 grant to verified accounts, no card, decided after task
  costs are measured and only if $0.50 cannot complete one representative Coach task. Sized so the
  eligible cohort fits funded capacity.
- **Annual plan rule**, written now, decided later: consider $120 a year once at least 50 payers
  have 90 days of observation, only if conservatively projected annual contribution is at least
  $48 after fees, refunds, allocated free usage and support, and the full-allowance stress case stays
  non-negative. Otherwise stay monthly. Annual retention benchmarks (44% yearly versus 17% monthly)
  do not prove that billing frequency causes retention.
- Show real remaining allowance and its reset time. Publish task-count estimates only after
  measuring them. Exhaustion preserves manual use and every existing result.
- Count unique, non-refunded payers. One person on three products is one subscriber.

## Stages and gates

**Stage 0, Nov 2 to Dec 20: $300 to $500 incremental cash.** $200 to $300 reserved for measured AI
use, $100 to $200 for production and delivery. No media. CEO attention capped at eight hours a week.
Unlocks the creator preflight only when stores and checkout work, task costs are measured, free
demonstrations are funded, and at least five genuine full-price buyers show repeat AI use.

**Stage 1, the Q5 learning campaign: $1,900 to $4,100 across December and January.** $1,500 to
$3,000 for creators, $300 to $1,000 AI reserve, up to $100 operations. Creator cash is released in
tranches, never automatically:

| Tranche | Cumulative | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | $500 | preflight passed |
| 2 | $1,500 | at least 100 attributed installs, 3 full-price purchases, 20% D7 workout activation, 10% applied-Coach activation |
| 3 | $3,000 | tranche-2 thresholds met independently by at least two creators, cost per D7-activated user at or below $5, projected fully loaded CAC at or below 2 × conservatively forecast C90 |

One thousand installs per channel give direction; they do not pick a winner between similar arms.

**Stage 2, $5,000 and more a month.** Requires at least 50 non-refunded payers across successive
cohorts from a repeatable channel, 70% first renewal, and fully loaded CAC at or below 1 × realised
C90. Allocate 70% to the proven channel, 20% to adjacent tests, 10% to retention and measurement.
Raise weekly commitments by at most 25%, pause after two cohorts over the limit, and replicate
outside Q5 before assuming holiday economics persist. At a $15 allowable CAC, $5,000 needs about 334
new payers a month; spend only if that volume exists.

**Two experiments at a time.** Build work (listings, cards, pages, instrumentation) is not an
experiment and runs in parallel through agents. December's experiments are the Gym cohort and
Roadmap discovery; Q5's are creators and sharing; the recap email and the Journal cohort follow on
January 16; the cross-product invitation no earlier than February 20.
