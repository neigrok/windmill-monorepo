# Windmill's iOS-first marketing strategy

**Build a discoverable Windmill presence while preparing the first public iOS release.** Windmill is one self-growth app with Roadmap, Journal and Gym. Lead demonstrations with a builder turning a project into a skill tree; give lifters and writers their own useful entry points. Each invitation has one job, one demonstrated outcome and one destination. The App Store listing represents the whole app.

The proposed operating cap is **eight founder hours per week and $0 incremental campaign spend**. Search repairs, useful original resources, truthful listings and regular distribution proceed alongside two small behavior experiments. A pilot's activation or return result decides whether to expand that experiment. Foundational publishing and discovery have their own quality and technical gates; record execution only when it happens.

## Launch contract

The [iOS release tree](https://windmill.works/app/roadmap#/app/t_4549f1f8cc783f1b) is the source of launch truth, read on November 17, 2026. The owner's scope is:

- The AI service, with Echoes' calls moved onto it under Windmill's own budgets.
- The shared Coach engine and shared Roadmap Coach capabilities.
- Roadmap records and rules on the shared sync engine.
- The native Roadmap room, including nested roadmaps, guides, deep links and Coach.
- Gym Coach running on the iPhone through the AI service.
- Live Journal Talk on the iPhone through the AI service.
- Windmill One sold inside the iOS app, including subscription review, entitlement and funded allowance, and the owner's decision to open the paid line.

**There is no launch date.** Derive a realistic release window from those dependencies. The web as a device runs alongside; Android, Google Play and Paddle do not gate this release. Marketing does not narrow the release scope to make an announcement date. A scope change belongs to the owner.

The available iOS beta carries Journal and Gym; that is not evidence that the planned three-room launch is available. Verify the exact build and storefront before promising native Roadmap, live Talk, the new Coach or a purchase. Current hosted-AI accounting is documented in [the usage review](../AI_USAGE_REVIEW.md); the new service and its integrations have their own [launch architecture](../foundation/ai-service.md). Do not turn today's allowance or server behavior into a promise about the upcoming service. The contract above describes launch requirements, not completed work.

## Two behavior experiments at a time

Start with the free [20-builder Roadmap cohort](reports/11-roadmap-seed-trees.md) and [10-lifter Gym cohort](reports/08-gym-demand-cohort.md). Their status is planned until actual recruitment is recorded. Before the first invitation, record intake start and close dates, first-action cutoff, source, surface, denominator and the final read date. Keep public iOS users in a new cohort. The launch does not wait for a prelaunch cohort to mature.

| Experiment | Continue | Rework | Observation clock |
|---|---|---|---|
| Roadmap | At least 8/20 activated and 4/20 returning | Fewer than four activate or fewer than two return | Activation within 48 hours of tree creation; account-backed return from day 7 inclusive to day 14 exclusive after signup. |
| Gym | At least 4/10 complete two qualifying sessions | 0–1/10 complete both | Two distinct finished workouts with at least five sets each within seven days of the first set. |

Intermediate results call for interviews and another same-size cohort. Nonstarters stay in the denominator; unknowns that can change the decision keep it pending. Keep creative and destination fixed within an intake, label assisted use and retain signed-out attempts. These small samples are learning rules, not estimates of cold-traffic conversion or market size. [Definitions and weekly review](funnels.md)

Recruit through existing contacts or relevant host-approved feedback threads. Offer a builder a useful starter and a lifter the log for workouts already planned. At follow-up ask “What did you actually use the next time?” and “Where did you stop?” Record bounded reasons, not personal stories. A host sharing a link is one placement, not an inferred audience count. [Community practice](reports/15-community-answering.md)

Repeat a passing cohort with an independent source. Rework the observed obstacle before expanding a failing invitation; continue useful foundational publishing meanwhile. Journal's [manual retention study](reports/13-journal-voice-cohort.md), [recap](reports/10-weekly-recap.md) and [cross-product invitation](reports/16-cross-product-invitation.md) need a free experiment slot. Journal resources and launch-required Talk do not wait for that research slot.

## When money enters the plan

Windmill One is sold first as a monthly App Store subscription inside the iOS app and goes on sale with the public iOS release. The web sells nothing; a Paddle checkout on the web and Google Play come later, by a future owner ruling, with no date. The paid line is currently closed, and manual work is free. Prepare the offer before release and measure genuine demand from the first day customers can buy in the App Store. A request for an invite, use of an external assistant, a free hosted Coach turn or a hypothetical price answer is evidence of interest. **A paid-demand test starts only when a customer can really buy**, the owner opens the paid line and approves funding, billing and refund behavior is verified, and hosted AI costs are measured. Customer-facing claims about AI must match the enabled allowance. [Billing and usage contract](../AI_USAGE_REVIEW.md) · [One offer](reports/04-gym-demo-and-checkout.md)

For the separate 50-lifter demand test, quote One's App Store price for the participant's storefront in invitations. Start that experiment once an eligible returning cohort and a test slot are available. Proposed learning gate: five genuine non-refunded buyers and at least half of those buyers performing a second useful Coach task in a later week. Keep an unobserved payer window pending. If this gate passes, price a single creator test with usage rights, a cash ceiling and a working store destination. Each commercial commitment is an owner decision.

For scale, retain the stricter gate: 50 unique non-refunded payers across successive cohorts from the same repeatable channel, at least 70% of eligible first renewals kept, and fully loaded acquisition cost at or below realised 90-day contribution. Include founder time, content and allocated free AI use. Any bounded learning spend before those cohorts mature needs its own owner-approved cap.
