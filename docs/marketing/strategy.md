# Windmill's iOS-first marketing strategy

**This is the plan for how people find Windmill, try it once and come back.** It is written for the owner. It says where Windmill stands, who is invited first, which ways of being found are allowed, how the first people are found, and which work is the owner's and which is an agent's. It records no work done. Work is recorded on the [marketing tree](https://windmill.works/#/app/t_970fc0a989207ce2) when it happens.

The proposed scale is **eight owner hours a week and $0 of campaign spending**. Eight hours is a ceiling on the owner's own time; agent work is not counted in it. Both numbers are the owner's to change. The [growth options report](reports/18-growth-options.md) compares 24 routes, larger ones included, and says what each would cost. Writing this plan sends nothing, publishes nothing, opens no account, builds nothing and spends nothing.

## Positioning

> Windmill is where a person keeps growing in body, skill and mind, with the machine working for them. Bring your own AI or use ours at a fair price. Nothing is locked and nothing is graded.

[Brand foundations](../design/brand-foundations.md#the-idea) is the one home of this sentence, the reason behind it, the meaning of the name and what Windmill never says. Marketing claims a pillar only as far as the rule or fact behind it reaches.

| Pillar | Standing rule or fact |
|---|---|
| The machine serves | Coach changes an existing routine only as a proposal the lifter applies, and after its first greeting it waits to be asked. [Gym principles](../design/brand-foundations.md#gym) |
| Yours and open | Everything done by hand works signed out. A person's own assistant reaches Roadmap and Gym through MCP; Journal has no MCP door, and Windmill takes no provider key or token from a person. [Accounts and sign-in](../design/brand-foundations.md#accounts-and-sign-in) · [Connect](https://windmill.works/connect.html) |
| Never graded | Journal has no streaks or scores and praises no value. Gym has no grades or streaks. The honesty rules allow no urgency on any surface. [Journal principles](../design/brand-foundations.md#journal) · [Numbers and money](../design/brand-foundations.md#numbers-and-money) |
| A fair price, shown | Not shown yet. The [pricing page](../../web/public/pricing.html) names $12 a month and no allowance amount, so it does not say where the money goes. [Owner decisions](#owner-decisions-the-positioning-opens) |

## What every channel keeps

- **Nothing that frightens or belittles.** No "AI will replace you", no "don't fall behind", no "fix yourself".
- **Publish only when there is something to say.** There is no posting cadence, and nothing is made to keep activity. Every piece shows real work or a real idea.
- **Work follows readiness.** No campaign is timed to a season or a launch date. Observation windows are measurement, not deadlines.
- **Brand-led, with at most one founder note.** Writing and publishing it are the owner's alone, and no agent drafts it. If it is published, it is in the first person under a pseudonym, tells why Windmill exists and what the name means, appears once, and is posted only where disclosed self-posts are allowed. Public creative and posts do not name or show the founder; a post still discloses that its author builds Windmill.
- **A master is paid for their time, never their verdict.** [Masters](reports/09-creator-campaign.md)
- **The honesty rules hold in every channel.** [Honesty](../design/brand-foundations.md#honesty)

## Launch contract

The [iOS release tree](https://windmill.works/app/roadmap#/app/t_4549f1f8cc783f1b) is the source of launch truth, read on November 17, 2026. The owner's scope is:

- The AI service, with Echoes' calls moved onto it under Windmill's own budgets.
- The shared Coach engine and shared Roadmap Coach capabilities.
- Roadmap records and rules on the shared sync engine.
- The native Roadmap room, including nested roadmaps, guides, deep links and Coach.
- Gym Coach running on the iPhone through the AI service.
- Live Journal Talk on the iPhone through the AI service.
- Windmill One sold inside the iOS app, including subscription review, entitlement and funded allowance, and the owner's decision to open the paid line.

**There is no launch date.** Derive a realistic release window from those dependencies. The web as a device runs alongside; Android, Google Play and Paddle do not gate this release. Marketing does not narrow the release scope to make an announcement date. A scope change belongs to the owner.

The available iOS beta carries Journal and Gym; that is not evidence that the planned three-room launch is available. Verify the exact build and storefront before promising native Roadmap, live Talk, the new Coach or a purchase. Current hosted-AI accounting is documented in [the usage review](../AI_USAGE_REVIEW.md); the new service and its integrations have their own [launch architecture](../foundation/ai-service.md). Do not turn today's allowance or server behavior into a promise about the upcoming service. The contract above describes launch requirements, not completed work.

## Who we invite first, and why

Windmill is for people in general. Nobody knows yet which door most of them will come through, so each entry point below is a test and never a label on the product.

A builder with a small project is the proposed first invitation, because that door was open to a stranger at the last check: on November 12, 2026 the [Ship v1 starter](reports/11-roadmap-seed-trees.md) opened in a browser, signed out, with nothing to install. The Gym door needs a beta invitation on a phone. The Journal door is open on the web, and its test waits for a free slot.

| Entry point | The person and the moment | What we show them | Where they go today |
|---|---|---|---|
| Roadmap, proposed first | A builder shipping a small project who needs to see what comes before what, and the next step. | “Turn your release checklist into a skill tree. Finish one step and see what opens next.” | [Roadmap](https://windmill.works/roadmap), then **Ship v1** in the [starter picker](https://windmill.works/roadmap#/app/start). After the release: the verified iPhone listing and the same task in the app. |
| Gym | A lifter repeating a routine who needs the last workout in front of them at the next session. | “Bring your routine. Log it on your phone and see what you lifted when you return.” | [Gym](https://windmill.works/gym) and a verified iPhone beta invitation; the public App Store install after the release. Android beta testers are counted as their own group. |
| Journal | A writer who wants to save a thought and find it later. | “Write a page today. Find it again when you need it.” | [Journal](https://windmill.works/journal) or a verified iPhone beta invitation. Live Talk is shown only after the release build and its allowance pass their checks. |

Each invitation has one job, one thing shown and one place to go. Say **Windmill Roadmap**, **Windmill Gym** or **Windmill Journal**, with **windmill.works**, at first mention. Descriptive terms should state the job: skill tree planner, workout log and private journal are candidates to check against the real page and build. Search volumes are never invented. Windmill is not the unrelated developer automation platform of the same name, and copy should leave no room for that confusion. An invitation to connect an assistant names one concrete job and points to the [connection guide](https://windmill.works/connect.html). The shared account is explained when a person wants another room. The App Store listing presents the whole app.

## Two behavior experiments at a time

Only two groups are watched at once. A group here is fixed: its people are invited once, nobody is added later, and those who never start still count. The reports call such a group a cohort.

Start with [20 builders on Roadmap](reports/11-roadmap-seed-trees.md) and [10 lifters on Gym](reports/08-gym-demand-cohort.md), both free. Both are planned and neither has started. Before the first invitation goes out, write down when inviting opens and closes, the last day a first action counts, where each person came from, which surface they use, and the day the result is read. People who install from the public App Store form a new group. The launch does not wait for an earlier group to finish.

| Experiment | Continue | Rework | The clock |
|---|---|---|---|
| Roadmap | At least 8 of 20 activated and 4 of 20 returning | Fewer than four activate or fewer than two return | Activated: the person owns a copy of the starter with at least three steps and completes one within 48 hours of creating it. Returning: an account-backed visit to their tree from day 7 inclusive to day 14 exclusive after signup. |
| Gym | At least 4 of 10 complete two qualifying sessions | 0 or 1 of 10 complete both | Two distinct finished workouts with at least five sets each, within seven days of the first set. |

A result between the two lines calls for interviews and another group of the same size. People who never start stay in the count. If a missing answer could change the decision, the decision waits. Within one group the invitation and the destination stay fixed, help given is noted, and signed-out attempts are kept. These groups are small. They show where people stop; they do not estimate how strangers will convert or how big a market is. [Definitions and weekly review](funnels.md)

A host who shares the guide brings people into the Roadmap group. That is one source inside the group, not a third experiment, and the same holds for the people who try the assistant connection. Once inviting has begun, the offer does not change; a new offer waits for the next group. A builder is offered a useful starter, and a lifter a log for workouts already planned. Afterwards each is asked “What did you actually use the next time?” and “Where did you stop?” Record short reasons, never personal stories. A host sharing a link counts as one placement, never as a guessed audience.

Repeat a passing group with people from a different source. When a group fails, fix what stopped people before inviting more; useful publishing continues meanwhile. Journal's [return study](reports/13-journal-voice-cohort.md), the [recap](reports/10-weekly-recap.md) and the [cross-product invitation](reports/16-cross-product-invitation.md) each need a free slot. Journal guides and the Talk the release requires do not wait for one.

## Finding the first people

Windmill has one regular user outside the build and no list of people to invite. Finding people is research, and agents do it. The owner approves what they find before anyone is contacted. No candidate list exists yet.

An agent returns named candidates of four kinds:

- **Places where the question Windmill answers is already being asked:** communities, forums, and the hosts of courses and clubs.
- **Masters by craft**, as [report 09](reports/09-creator-campaign.md) defines one: lifting first, engineering for Roadmap second.
- **Directories and editors** that accept a product like this one.
- **Public questions already asked** that an honest answer could serve, under the disclosure rule in [report 15](reports/15-community-answering.md).

Each candidate carries four things: a link to the evidence, why it fits, that place's own rule about self-promotion, and the smallest honest first step. A candidate without evidence is left off the list. An audience size is written only where the place publishes one.

The order of work:

1. Agents research and hand the owner one list.
2. The owner approves, strikes or adds names. People the owner already knows are his to add and his to invite.
3. For each approved candidate, an agent drafts the message on behalf of the product, in the product's voice, with the disclosure that place requires.
4. Nothing is sent until the owner approves that message. One approval covers one message to one place.
5. Anything personal is the owner's to write: an invitation to someone he knows, a maker's note, the founder note.
6. A refusal or a moderator's no ends that placement. There are no bulk messages and no unsolicited direct messages.

People who accept join one of the [two groups](#two-behavior-experiments-at-a-time), with their source recorded.

## What the owner does and what agents do

Agents write and speak on behalf of the product. They never write, send or publish the owner's own story, or anything signed as the owner.

Every action is one of three kinds, by whom it needs:

| Mark | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Only what needs the person: a ruling, money, an account or legal identity only he holds, a personal relationship, his own writing, or pressing Publish or Send on something public. |
| **Agent** | An agent can finish it alone with the repository and its tools: research, analysis, drafting for the product, building pages or measurement, and reading results. Anything a person sees is built only from a design the owner has approved. |
| **Agent, owner approves** | An agent does the work, and one approval from the owner releases it: sending, publishing, spending, or marking a design Ready for dev. |

Each owner action is cut down to the decision itself: an agent prepares it, and the owner decides. The actions live on the [marketing tree](https://windmill.works/#/app/t_970fc0a989207ce2), one task per node. There an approval is its own Owner node that waits on the agent's node. Marking an action for an agent does not start it. A task is done when its node records the evidence.

## When money enters the plan

Windmill One is the fair price for Windmill-funded AI and for the work of making Windmill, and it keeps Windmill independent. Copy may say so plainly. A person can use their own assistant instead, which costs Windmill nothing and earns nothing. Using Windmill and paying nothing is a good outcome. Expect trust before revenue: a person who already pays for an assistant can connect it to Roadmap and Gym and never need One there.

Windmill One is sold first as a monthly App Store subscription inside the iOS app and goes on sale with the public iOS release. The web sells nothing; a Paddle checkout on the web and Google Play come later, by a future owner ruling, with no date. The paid line is currently closed, and manual work is free. Prepare the offer before release and measure genuine demand from the first day customers can buy in the App Store. A request for an invite, use of an external assistant, a free hosted Coach turn or a hypothetical price answer is evidence of interest. **A paid-demand test starts only when a customer can really buy**, the owner opens the paid line and approves funding, billing and refund behavior is verified, and hosted AI costs are measured. Customer-facing claims about AI must match the enabled allowance. [Billing and usage contract](../AI_USAGE_REVIEW.md) · [One offer](reports/04-gym-demo-and-checkout.md)

For the separate 50-lifter demand test, quote One's App Store price for the participant's storefront in invitations. Start that experiment once an eligible returning cohort and a test slot are available. Proposed learning gate: five genuine non-refunded buyers and at least half of those buyers performing a second useful Coach task in a later week. Keep an unobserved payer window pending. If this gate passes, price one master's placement under [the masters rules](reports/09-creator-campaign.md): a flat disclosed fee, a cash ceiling and a working store destination. Each commercial commitment is an owner decision.

For scale, retain the stricter gate: 50 unique non-refunded payers across successive cohorts from the same repeatable channel, at least 70% of eligible first renewals kept, and fully loaded acquisition cost at or below realised 90-day contribution. Include founder time, content and allocated free AI use. Any bounded learning spend before those cohorts mature needs its own owner-approved cap. ## Owner decisions the positioning opens

- Whether the pricing page states where the $12 goes: the AI allowance, the store's fee, running Windmill and the work. It states none of this today.
- The cash ceiling for a master's fee, and whether the fee is owed when the master publishes nothing. No fee benchmark is recorded in [the evidence](benchmarks.md), and no ceiling is approved.
- Whether Windmill will take a person's own provider key or token. It takes none today.
