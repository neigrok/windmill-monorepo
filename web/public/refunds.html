Refunds

Updated November 2026

Windmill One is not on sale yet. It will be sold first in the iPhone app.

The store you buy it in takes the payment and handles cancelling and refunds.

Nothing you made is deleted when a subscription ends: you return to the free amount.

Once it is on sale, Windmill One renews each month until you cancel it in that store.

Nothing that is free today will move behind a plan later, privacy included.

Nothing on these pages limits the rights the law gives you.
