Privacy

Updated July 2026

The short version: signed out, your trees live on your device — we never see them. Signing in syncs them to your account so they follow you — and that’s the main reason we store anything.

What we store

  • Signed out: your trees stay in this browser, on this device — we never see them. What we do keep is anonymous usage events under a random per-browser key, so we can tell what’s working. No names, no tree content.
  • Signed in: your email address, the trees you sync, your active sessions, and any AI-tool connections you’ve granted — plus those same usage events, now tied to your account. That’s the list.

Signing in

Windmill uses magic links — there are no passwords, so there is nothing to leak or reuse. Each link works once and lasts 15 minutes. Sessions last 90 days per device; see and revoke every one, or sign out everywhere, from account settings.

Email we send

One kind: sign-in links you ask for — including the variant that plants your copy of a shared tree. That’s every email we send today. We don’t send marketing you didn’t ask for.

Sharing

A share link makes that tree’s page visible to anyone holding the link, and anyone holding it can fork their own copy — progress cleared.

Measurement

We look at product usage in aggregate to understand what’s working. No ad trackers, no selling data, no third-party profiles.

Who processes it with us

A few outside companies each handle one specific job, and only ever see the data that job needs:

  • Paddle — our seller of record for Windmill One. If you subscribe, Paddle takes the payment and handles tax; your billing details go to them, and we never see your card.
  • Resend — delivers the sign-in emails you ask for. It receives your email address to send the link, and nothing else.
  • Anthropic — powers tending, the AI that plants and reshapes a tree. When you tend a tree, that tree and your instruction are sent to Anthropic to generate the edit — only when you ask for one, and they don’t use it to train their models.

None of them profile you, and none of them receive your data to sell it on.

Your data is portable

Export every tree as a zip of Markdown from account settings — the same format you can paste back to replant. Closing your account there starts a 30-day grace (signing in again undoes it); after it, your synced copies go. Trees on your devices always stay yours.
