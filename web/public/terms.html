Terms

Updated August 2026

The short version: what you make is yours, be decent with sharing, and remember Windmill is a beta — it will keep changing under you, hopefully for the better.

The service

Windmill is three products behind one account: a roadmap that draws a plan as a skill tree, a journal you write in at night, and a training log. It’s in public beta: features move, and occasionally something you liked changes shape. Material changes land in the changelog.

What you make is yours

You own everything you make here — your trees, your journal pages, your training log. By syncing or sharing any of it you give us just enough license to host it and show it where you chose to show it — nothing more, and it ends when you delete it.

Sharing & forking

A share link makes a tree’s page visible to anyone with the link, and anyone viewing it may fork a copy — structure, names, and kinds, with progress reset. Don’t share what you don’t have the right to share.

Acceptable use

No illegal content, no harassment, no using share pages as a spam or malware channel. We can take down pages that cross those lines; we’ll tell you when we do.

Accounts

Sign-in is by magic link to your email, so keep that address yours and current. You’re responsible for what happens in your sessions; revoke any of them, or close your account, from account settings.

Paying for the AI

Building, sharing, exporting, and keeping a tree private are all free — see pricing. What will cost money is the AI doing the work: tending in the roadmap, Talk and the full text of an echo in the journal. Coach in the gym answers ten questions a day without a plan; a plan only raises the monthly AI ceiling behind it. Nothing is on sale today — Windmill has no checkout, no card is asked for anywhere, and tending is not switched on at all. Windmill One is planned at $12 a month; when it opens, nothing that is free today moves behind it.

When Windmill One opens it will be billed in US dollars before any local sales tax or VAT, and renew monthly until cancelled. There is no cancel button in account settings yet; until there is, write to hello@windmill.works and we’ll stop the renewal. Either way the subscription runs to the end of the period you’ve paid for and doesn’t renew after it. If the price changes, it changes for new subscriptions first and we’ll email you before it affects yours.

Payment is taken by Paddle, who act as the seller of record — their buyer terms apply to the transaction alongside these, and your receipt and card statement will show Paddle rather than Windmill. There’s a 30-day money-back guarantee; the whole of it is in the refund policy. Lapsing never deletes anything you made. What stops is the AI: Talk goes quiet and a journal echo shortens back to its opening words. Coach in the gym keeps answering its ten questions a day, within the ceiling every account has. Every page, every tree and every set stays exactly where it was.

No warranties, in plain words

Windmill is provided as-is. We work to keep it fast, safe, and durable — local-first by design, so your work survives us having a bad day — but we can’t promise uninterrupted service, and our liability is limited to what you’ve paid us in the twelve months before whatever went wrong. Nothing here limits rights your local consumer law gives you and doesn’t let us sign away.

Changes to these terms

If these terms change in a way that matters, we’ll email account holders and note it in the changelog before it takes effect.

Questions

Write to hello@windmill.works — a person reads it.
