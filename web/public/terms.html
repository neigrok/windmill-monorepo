Terms

Updated July 2026

The short version: your trees are yours, be decent with sharing, and remember Windmill is a beta — it will keep changing under you, hopefully for the better.

The service

Windmill turns roadmaps into skill trees you can track and share. It’s in public beta: features move, and occasionally something you liked changes shape. Material changes land in the changelog.

Your trees are yours

You own everything you make here. By syncing or sharing a tree you give us just enough license to host it and show it to the people you share it with — nothing more, and it ends when you delete it.

Sharing & forking

A share link makes a tree’s page visible to anyone with the link, and anyone viewing it may fork a copy — structure, names, and kinds, with progress reset. Don’t share what you don’t have the right to share.

Acceptable use

No illegal content, no harassment, no using share pages as a spam or malware channel. We can take down pages that cross those lines; we’ll tell you when we do.

Accounts

Sign-in is by magic link to your email, so keep that address yours and current. You’re responsible for what happens in your sessions; revoke any of them, or close your account, from account settings.

Paying for tending

Building, sharing, exporting, and keeping a tree private are all free — see pricing. The one thing that costs money is tending: the AI that plants and reshapes your tree for you. Every account gets a monthly allowance of tendings for free; Windmill Pro is $12 a month for a larger one. Nothing that is free today moves behind the paywall.

Pro is billed in US dollars before any local sales tax or VAT, renews monthly until you cancel, and can be cancelled any time from account settings — the subscription runs to the end of the period you’ve paid for and doesn’t renew after it. If the price changes, it changes for new subscriptions first and we’ll email you before it affects yours.

Payment is taken by Paddle, who act as the seller of record — their buyer terms apply to the transaction alongside these, and your receipt and card statement will show Paddle rather than Windmill. There’s a 30-day money-back guarantee; the whole of it is in the refund policy. Lapsing never deletes anything you made — you simply stop earning new tendings until you’re back on Pro.

No warranties, in plain words

Windmill is provided as-is. We work to keep it fast, safe, and durable — local-first by design, so your work survives us having a bad day — but we can’t promise uninterrupted service, and our liability is limited to what you’ve paid us in the twelve months before whatever went wrong. Nothing here limits rights your local consumer law gives you and doesn’t let us sign away.

Changes to these terms

If these terms change in a way that matters, we’ll email account holders and note it in the changelog before it takes effect.
