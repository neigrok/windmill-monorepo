The fine print, said plainly

No surprises on the receipt.

Who you pay

Windmill uses Paddle as its seller of record. Paddle runs the checkout and handles tax; your receipt comes from Paddle, not from us.

What it costs

$12 per month, in US dollars, before local sales tax or VAT. One plan — 300 tendings a month, plus the AI in the journal and the gym. No tiers, no per-run pricing to forecast.

When you run out

Tending pauses when your monthly allowance is used — 30 on Free, 300 on Windmill One — but hand editing stays free and your trees are untouched. Allowances reset each month.

Changing your mind

A 30-day money-back guarantee: within 30 days, email hello@windmill.works and we refund it. After that, the same address stops the next renewal — self-serve cancellation from Settings is not built yet — and you keep Windmill One to the end of the period you’ve paid for. Full refund policy →
