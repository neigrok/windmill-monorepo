Questions

Is Windmill really free?
Yes. Everything you do by hand is free, and so is connecting your own AI.
Can I buy Windmill One today?
No. It is not on sale yet.
What happens when my AI amount runs out?
Windmill’s AI pauses until the month turns. Everything by hand keeps working.
Do echoes use the AI amount?
No. Windmill pays for echoes, and they do not count against it.
Can I use my own AI instead?
Yes, with Roadmap and Gym, at no charge from Windmill. Journal has no door for an outside assistant.
Who takes the payment?
The store you buy it in. Cancelling and refunds go through that store. It will be sold first in the iPhone app.
Is there a yearly plan or a trial?
No yearly plan. The free monthly amount is the trial.
If I stop paying, do I lose anything?
No. Your trees, pages and logs stay, and you return to the free amount.
