Support

Windmill is three tools in one account: Windmill Roadmap, Windmill Journal and Windmill Gym.

Write to us

Email hello@windmill.works and say what you did and what you saw. The Feedback button at the foot of the front page reaches the same team; leave your email there if you’d like a reply.

Your data

Signed out, your trees, journal pages and workout log are kept on the device you made them on: in this browser on the web, or in the app on a phone. Signing in syncs them to your account, so they follow you.

To keep a copy, open Settings on the web. Export my trees saves a .zip of Markdown, one file per tree, and Export my pages saves your journal as one Markdown file. Both work signed in or signed out. The workout log has no export. The phone apps have none either: sign in on the phone, then export from the web.

To delete your account on the web, open Settings: it ends with Close your account, where you choose Close my account and type your email to confirm. In the phone apps, open You, choose Delete account and type your email to confirm. Either way every device signs out at once, and every connected tool loses its access. For 30 days, signing in again brings it all back; after that it is deleted for good.
